About Us

Discover Adarsh Group

Adarsh Industries was started in 1990. Its foundation was laid by the Honorable Chairman Shri Narendra Kumar Mittal. Further the company was taken over by his son, The Managing Director of the company Mr. Puneet Mittal.

At Adarsh Industries, they specialize in providing high-quality industrial electrical appliances to meet the diverse needs of modern industries. With a commitment to excellence and innovation, we strive to deliver cutting-edge solutions that enhance efficiency, safety, and productivity in industrial environments.

Adarsh Industries has a team of more than 100 members of highly qualified engineers. Our mission is to be a trusted partner for businesses seeking reliable electrical appliances for their industrial operations.
“Our vision is to lead with purpose, integrity, and innovation. We aim to be a beacon of excellence in our industry, driving sustainable growth while making a positive impact on society.”
Puneet Mittal — Chairman
